    Our local game variant

    A six releases a token and grants another roll. Three consecutive sixes forfeit the third roll; earlier moves remain. Stars and colored start squares are safe. Landing on an opponent on another shared square sends that token to its yard. Stacks do not form blockades; all opposing tokens on an unsafe destination are captured. An exact roll is needed to finish. Captures and finishing do not grant extra rolls. Two-player mode uses Red and Yellow.

    Graphics you can point to

    The board uses authored vertex and fragment shaders, texture sampling, Lambert diffuse lighting, a Blinn–Phong varnish highlight, shadow sampling, a time-based turn pulse, and texture blending. Every rendered mesh and sprite has a procedural canvas texture. The scene uses a perspective camera, hemispheric/ambient/directional lighting, soft shadows, and time-based animations.

    Presentation demo

    Switch on the separate demo scene. Choose 6 and move Red token 2 to demonstrate a capture. Start the demo again, choose 3, and move Red token 4 to demonstrate exact finishing. Leaving the demo restores your normal game.
